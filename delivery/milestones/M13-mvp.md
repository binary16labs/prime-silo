# M13 — MVP: the owner can run their estate from the app → **tag unset**

**Scope (signed by the owner 2026-10-17; simplified on the owner's direction 2026-10-18, "we are
overcomplicating this and we need to simplify to amplify").** The signed version had five exit
criteria. Four of them turned out to measure nothing, and my answer to that was a recovery plan
with four instrument-building jobs — a ruler to check a rubric to gate a release, three layers
above the product. That was the overcomplication, and it is withdrawn.

## The one outcome

> The owner opens Prime-Silo on the T480, **sees what is on their drives**, asks Benny a question
> about it and gets an answer that cites where it came from, approves something, and can show
> afterwards who approved it. **No terminal, start to finish.**

## Done is one walk-through

Done is that journey walked **once, end to end, with the owner at the keyboard**. Not a gate, not
a coverage percentage, not a scorecard. If it works, it is finished; if it stops somewhere, that
stop is the next piece of work and nothing else is.

There is no instrument because the instrument was the problem. A journey a person completes is
stronger evidence than any number I can compute about it, and it cannot be satisfied by editing a
JSON field.

## Out — and this is the whole point

Out of the MVP, in addition to the earlier out-list (EP-T, EP-M, EP-D, EP-R, the book pipeline,
ASUS burst, EP-U U5–U10):

- **Self-publishing (was MVP-5).** How the owner gets updates is not why the product exists. One
  release by hand is fine.
- **A clean evidence-pack verdict (was MVP-4).** The two unauthorised runs are orphans with no
  proposal, so they can never be signed and the count can never reach zero. They stay **named in
  the pack as a known, closed defect** — that is what an honest append-only ledger looks like — and
  they stop being a release gate. This replaces the amendment I was going to ask the owner to sign.
- **Use-case coverage as a number (was MVP-2).** 19 counted journeys, measured by a hand-typed
  word, is bookkeeping. One real walk-through replaces it.
- **A first-install gate (was MVP-1).** The walk-through is the test.
