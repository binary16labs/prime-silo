# B4 — Delivery board → the B0 ledger (one source of truth)

## Goal

Board transitions become ledger events. `BOARD.md` stops being a second source of truth and
becomes a **projection** of the B0 hash-chained ledger, carrying W1's `task_verified` in the same shape
rather than being retrofitted later. This is B2's explicitly deferred follow-up, and the first of three
open hops between a delivery decision and the lineage DAG.

## TDD plan

1. Red: a claim recorded through the delivery loop appears as a ledger event, not only a board edit.

3. Red: a board/ledger disagreement becomes structurally impossible — one source, one writer.
