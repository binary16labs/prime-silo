# S7 — the governed executor: a signed move, verified before anything is removed

## Goal

The Prime-Silo node that owns the bytes executes a signed centralisation proposal: restartable copy to the
destination, destination hash verified against the source, `artifact_placed` at the destination, and only then
`artifact_evicted` at the source. The NAS catalog records the arrival directly, so client receipts are no longer
needed. **Human-signed** because it moves data.

## TDD plan

1. No signature, a declined proposal, or a proposal signed for a different content set: nothing is written, exit non-zero.
2. Copy is restartable; an interrupted copy resumes and the result is verified by full hash.
3. Destination hash mismatch: the destination copy is quarantined, the source is untouched, the failure is an event.
4. Re-applying a completed proposal is a no-op (idempotent).

## Out of scope

Deleting duplicates without a destination copy; moves across machines that are not both online and registered.

## Handoff

Verifier runs the gate, then with owner signature centralises one real models folder from optimus to the NAS and
records bytes, time and the verification events in LOG.

## Amendment — consolidating backup drives (owner directive 2026-09-21)

This executor carries out the owner's consolidation (directive 2026-09-21), under **report only, I
decide each**: every batch is a separately signed proposal, and the executor never widens a
signature to content the owner did not see.

**A removal needs a copy that exists NOW, on an attached device.** The "Out of scope" line above
excludes machines that are not both online; the same rule binds local drives, which come and go. The
executor re-verifies the surviving copy by full hash, on an attached device, immediately before it
evicts any other copy. A surviving copy on a device that is not attached, or whose hash cannot be
read at that moment, aborts the eviction and leaves every copy in place — the proposal is not
failed, it is left for when the drive is back.

**Eviction is the last step and is never a batch shortcut.** Per content: verify survivor, record
`artifact_placed` for the survivor if it moved, then evict others one at a time, each preceded by
its own verification. A failure part-way leaves everything not yet evicted intact.

Two further Scenarios, both required:

```gherkin
  Scenario: an unplugged survivor stops the eviction
    Given a signed proposal whose surviving copy is on a drive that is not attached
    When the executor runs it
    Then nothing is evicted, every copy remains, and the proposal is left runnable

  Scenario: verification immediately precedes each eviction
    Given a signed proposal to evict three redundant copies
    When the survivor's bytes change after the second eviction
    Then the third eviction does not happen and the change is recorded
```
