# F7 — Backup & restore of PRIME_SILO_HOME

## Goal
`benny home snapshot` produces a dated, hash-manifested archive of PRIME_SILO_HOME (graphs,
ledger, runs, knowledge, config — secrets/keystore EXCLUDED and listed as excluded);
`benny home restore <snapshot>` verifies every hash before touching anything and refuses partial
restores. Setup exposes both + a scheduled-backup toggle (an F3 schedule to a user-chosen destination).
