# F5 — Honest usage meter (+ sustainability accounting)

## Goal

The real version of the deleted website calculator, inside the product: fold G0 telemetry into
per-day/per-workflow/per-model totals — local vs cloud tokens, wall time, runs, wedges — rendered
in Observe, exportable as schema-valid JSON. Plus energy estimates (local: wall time × benchmark
tok/s × recorded power figure; cloud: cited per-token intensity), always labeled ESTIMATE with
methodology one click away.

- Plan §9.5 F5 card (PLAN-local-power-unified-ui.md:508-511)

- Cache hits (A1) metered as saved work, never hidden; retention budgets rendered honestly (§1.6)

## TDD plan

1. Red: synthetic event stream with known totals folds to exactly those totals.
2. Red: energy fold on a synthetic stream with known parameters reproduces hand-computed watt-hours.
3. Implement folds + view + export — green. 4. Local-vs-cloud split matches the router ledger for a real A2 pack run.
