# P3 — Ledger, lineage, serialisation, wedge detection

## Goal

OpenLineage RunEvent per bench; capture serving topology (R11); serialise subjects because the eGPU is
single-tenant; and detect a wedged endpoint by **CPU-time and artifact mtime, never a log line** (R12).
