# S4 — the Estate Map panel in the Prime-Silo UI

## Goal

A Prime-Silo panel `_prime_silo/estate_map`, registered like the Release panel, that renders the folded estate
map from `/api/estate/map*` only: machine cards (online, Prime-Silo version, volumes, map age), a storage treemap
(machine -> volume -> category), an applications-by-machine matrix with version drift highlighted, and media
roots. This replaces running report scripts to see the estate.

## Context pointers (never inline content)

- `architecture/SOLUTION-estate-map.md` §5
- `app/L0/_all/mod/_prime_silo/release/` (panel yaml, view.html, js, css pattern)
- `server/pages/estate.html` (EP-N console: theme and motion conventions)

## TDD plan

1. Panel yaml registers `_prime_silo/estate_map`; the view loads with the API mocked.
2. Treemap layout is a pure function of fold data (tested without a DOM); areas sum to used bytes.
3. Stale machines render as stale with their age; they are never hidden or shown as empty.
4. Version drift: two machines on different Prime-Silo versions are flagged in the matrix.

## Amendment — budget 600 to 900 (owner-signed 2026-09-26)

600 was my own estimate at authoring time and it was wrong by 40%. Measured at submission: 858
non-test lines — `fold_view.js` 267 (the pure judgements: squarified treemap, staleness, derived
per-volume sizes, version drift), `estate_map.css` 294 (a full panel stylesheet, token-only under
C0, comparable to the Release panel's), `view.html` 134, `estate_map.js` 89, `scripts/gates/s4.mjs`
67, `ext/panels/estate_map.yaml` 7.

Nothing here is padding I can find. The owner was offered the alternative — split the storage
treemap into its own contract and ship S4 without that tab — and chose to raise the budget rather
than narrow the panel, so the whole panel ships. The one thing not on the table was trimming the
judgements or their comments to fit a number I had guessed: "never relax a fix to fit a line
budget" applies to the comments that say what a guard actually does just as much as to the guard.

## Amendment — after the FAIL: budget 900 to 1500, allowlist + package.json (owner-signed 2026-09-26)

`claude-s4-verifier` FAILED the first submission with five blocking findings, and the owner chose to
fix all five in this contract rather than split any out. Two of them change what S4 is:

**B1 — the treemap was inventing numbers.** It split a machine's reported bytes across its volumes by
file share, which drew a small busy drive as the big one. The wire carries bytes per MACHINE and
files per VOLUME and no per-volume bytes at all, so no honest per-volume byte figure exists. The
treemap is now two nested levels in two units, labelled: machines by bytes, volumes by file count.
**The plan's §5 treemap (machine → volume → category, by bytes) is not deliverable from this wire**
— the node knows its per-volume bytes at sweep time and does not send them. That gap belongs to a
follow-up contract against S0/S3's payload, not to an estimate here.

**B4 — no test had ever executed the markup.** Five deleted guards shipped green at 19/19. The owner
chose jsdom over a vendored renderer or a declared limit, which is why `package.json` and
`package-lock.json` join the allowlist: adding a devDependency necessarily edits both. The panel's
mutually exclusive states also moved from `x-show` to `x-if`, so a state that does not apply is
ABSENT rather than hidden — a stronger statement, and what the tests now assert.

**Budget:** measured at resubmission, 1230 authored non-test lines of 1500. The `npm install` also
rewrote 544 lines of `package-lock.json`; that is generated, not authored, and is declared here
rather than counted, because a line budget is a budget on judgement, not on lockfiles.
