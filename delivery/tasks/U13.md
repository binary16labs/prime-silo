# U13 — a proven offline copy of F:

**Directed by the owner 2026-11-15** ("I want a backup of the F, that is the point: data
sovereignty"; target chosen: "New offline drive"). Brings forward the "offline mirror of F:" that
`SOLUTION-consolidation.md` §14 question 1 recommended for after U6–U8. Band M, 500 lines.

## Goal

F: (4.66 TB, 1.45 TB used) holds the estate's only copy. The owner gets a second copy on a drive
they own, kept offline, and **proof** that it holds everything: every file on F:, matched by full
fingerprint against F:'s catalogue. All of it runs from the Library tab, never from scripts.

## Context pointers (never inline content)

- `architecture/SOLUTION-consolidation.md` §8 (sole copies), §14 q1 (the mirror)
- `delivery/tasks/U1.md` (offered drives, catalogue placement, Catalogue my data), `U0.md` (registry)

## Design

1. **The owner names the mirror.** In the Drives tab, one press marks an attached removable drive as
   **F:'s mirror** (stored by identity in the registry, never by letter). One mirror for now.
2. **Back up F:** (Library tab, shown only while the mirror is attached). It copies F: onto
   `<mirror>/F-mirror/`, keeping paths. It copies new and changed files only (size + date), skips
   `System Volume Information` and `$RECYCLE.BIN`, and **never deletes** on the mirror. Files gone
   from F: are listed for the owner, not removed. Live catalogues and logs under the estate store
   and BENNY_HOME are copied as consistent snapshots (`VACUUM INTO`), never as open files. One pass
   at a time, through the hub's single librarian flight; Stop keeps what was copied.
3. **Proof.** The pass ends by cataloguing the mirror (U1's librarian, store placement). Then
   **coverage**: every F: catalogue row (path, size, sha256) is looked up on the mirror. The Library
   line reads "F: on the mirror: 100% (1,117,0xx files, 1.45 TB), checked <date>", or names the gap
   ("214 files, 3.2 GB missing or different": counts, with a local CSV). A mirror not attached is
   shown with its last proof and date, never as current coverage.
4. **Free space first.** The copy refuses to start if the mirror cannot hold what is missing, and
   says how much more is needed.
5. **Encryption is the owner's.** BitLocker To Go on the mirror, set up by the owner. The app never
   handles its password or recovery key.

## Pre-registered criteria (real T480, after the owner attaches the mirror)

- First copy of F: completes; the proof reads 100% of F: catalogue rows on the mirror.
- A refresh with nothing changed copies 0 files.

## Owner questions

1. **The drive.** 4–5 TB USB, the same size as F: (5 TB) preferred so it never runs short. Kept
   unplugged and, ideally, away from the T480.
2. **How often.** Recommendation: a refresh after each big change (a backup drive merged onto F:,
   a Downloads clear-out) and at least monthly; the Library line says how old the proof is.

## Out of scope

Copies across machines (U2–U4), retirement certificates (U9), the Pi as a second copy (later), bit-rot scrubbing.
