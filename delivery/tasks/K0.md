# K0 — the capacity report: how much data there really is to manage, before anything is bought

## Goal

The owner opens the Estate Map's **Capacity** tab and reads, in plain numbers:
- how much data they have, how much of it is unique, and of what kinds;
- how fast it has been growing;
- how much storage the primary store and each replica store need for 1, 2 and 3 years.

They decide what to buy from that page, not from a guess (EP-K, owner direction 2026-10-11). It is a
**report only**: it reads catalogues and the drive registry, never a file's contents, and changes
nothing. Every figure it cannot know exactly is shown as a **proven range** with the reason, never a
falsely precise single number. A preliminary report covers this machine's catalogues as soon as U1
has run. The estate-wide report arrives when U6's content index exists (Design point 8).

## Why (measured, 2026-10-11)

- F: is a 5.0 TB drive with 1.56 TB used. Its unique content is between 1.03 TB (certain: files
  whose size nothing else on F: has) and 1.55 TB.
- C: has 453 of 511 GB used, of which the owner's data is 136 GB in known folders plus 55 GB in the
  rest of OneDrive.
- Text documents are about 25,000 files and 1.3 GB. Media is about 90% of F:.
- Not measured: the ASUS, the backup drives, the Pi, the overlap between C: and F:, and growth.
  The owner wants to buy a setup and needs these numbers first.

## Design

1. **Sources.** The local catalogues (every drive and user root on this machine, U1) and U0's
   registry. Sources are an interface: U6 adds the estate content index as a source later, without
   changing the report.
2. **Totals, by what is known.** Per drive and user root, and in all: files and bytes, by kind
   (S23's kinds) and by scope.
   - **Rebuildable, tiny, junk and online-only** bytes are reported separately and never counted
     as data to protect.
   - **Owner content** is the planning figure.
3. **Unique content as a proven range.**
   - **Lower bound (certain):** bytes in sizes no other owner file in any source has, plus bytes
     proven distinct by full fingerprint.
   - **Upper bound:** owner bytes minus bytes proven duplicate by full fingerprint (S21/S23
     duplicate sets, and cross-catalogue fingerprint matches among this machine's catalogues).
   - The gap between them is named ("size-sharers not yet fingerprinted: N files, X GB"), together
     with what would close it (U5's estate levels, or a comparison).
4. **Uncatalogued drives are counted, not ignored.** A drive in the registry with no finished
   catalogue (for example a backup drive plugged in once) adds its **used space to the upper bound
   only**, listed by name (label withheld if a rule matches) with "not catalogued: up to X GB". An
   unattached drive is never assumed empty.
5. **Growth, honestly labelled.**
   - **Measured growth:** from catalogue pass history (owner bytes at each finished pass), once
     there are two or more passes at least 30 days apart; otherwise "not yet measurable".
   - **Accumulation history (a proxy, labelled as one):** owner bytes by year of capture time (S24
     EXIF) for photos, and of modified time for everything else, showing how fast the collection
     has grown over the years.
   - The projection uses measured growth when it exists, otherwise the proxy's last three years,
     and says which one it used.
6. **Projection for the purchase.** For 1, 2 and 3 years:
   - **needed usable capacity** = unique upper bound × projected growth ÷ (1 − free-space headroom);
   - per store role: the primary store and each replica store (EP-U store roles), each against its
     drive's current capacity, giving "fits for N years" or "short by X TB";
   - inputs editable on the page: copies (the policy's minimum, default 2), years, headroom
     (default 20% free), growth figure; the defaults are owner question 1;
   - **purchasing shapes** as capacities, never products or prices: "keep F: as primary and add a
     replica of ≥ X TB", or "a mirrored pair of ≥ X TB each as primary, with F: as replica";
     each with the years it lasts.
7. **The knowledge layer's cost, estimated.**
   - From document counts and sizes (owner scope, quarantined excluded): extracted text, chunks,
     vector storage at the configured embedding size, PageIndex trees and graph nodes, in GB and
     hours of eGPU time at the configured throughput.
   - Photo understanding (K6) is a separate line: images × seconds per image, in days.
   - All of these are labelled estimates, with their inputs shown.
8. **Preliminary, then estate-wide.** Until U6 exists, the report's header reads "This machine
   only: the ASUS, the Pi and uncatalogued drives are not in these numbers", and lists what is
   missing. When U6's content index is present, it is used as a source, and copies and replica
   coverage come from it.
9. **Computed off the hub's thread,** in a background worker, cached per catalogue state and policy,
   like S21's summary. It never reads a file and never writes anything but its own cache.
10. **Where it shows.** A **Capacity** tab in the Estate Map, with a "Download the report" link
    (Markdown, with the same numbers and inputs). `GET /api/estate/capacity` answers loopback only.
    Totals only, except per-drive and per-root lines on this machine, judged by the quarantine
    rules.

   - **Totals:** totals and kinds; rebuildable, tiny, junk and online-only separated from owner
     content.
   - **Bounds:**
     - the lower bound counts only unique sizes and proven-distinct bytes;
     - the upper bound subtracts only proven duplicates;
     - a cross-catalogue full-fingerprint match is subtracted once;
     - the gap is named with its file count;
     - the true unique size of each fixture lies within the bounds.
   - **Uncatalogued drives:** an uncatalogued drive adds its used space to the upper bound only; an
     unattached, uncatalogued drive is listed, never zero.
   - **Growth:** "not yet measurable" with one pass; measured with two passes 30 or more days
     apart; the proxy from EXIF and mtime years, labelled as a proxy.
   - **Projection:** the formula over a known input; "fits for N years" and "short by X TB"
     against store capacities; editable inputs change only the projection.
   - **Knowledge estimate:** recomputed from document counts with quarantined files excluded.
   - **Privacy and reads:** no quarantined name in the report, API or download; 0 file reads (a
     read spy over the whole computation).
2. Panel: the Capacity tab renders the header (preliminary or estate-wide), ranges, growth,
   projection and shapes through the real view; editing an input recomputes the projection.
3. Gate `k0.mjs`: the K0 tests, then the U1 gate (which runs the U0, S23, S22 and S21 gates).

## Pre-registered criteria (real T480, verifier, reported either way)

- With F: and the C: user roots catalogued (U1), the report is worked out in ≤ 5 min, and the hub
  stays responsive throughout (event-loop lag ≤ 100 ms).
- F:'s totals reconcile with Windows' used space (1.56 TB on 2026-10-11) once skipped folders, the
  estate store and NTFS overhead are shown. The difference is named, never hidden.
- F:'s unique lower bound is at least the 1.03 TB of unique-size bytes measured on 2026-10-11, and
  the upper bound is at most F:'s owner bytes.
- 0 file reads during the report (read spy); 0 quarantined names in the API, page and download
  (byte scan).

## Owner questions (answer at signature)

1. **Planning defaults.** Recommendation: 2 copies (primary plus one replica store), 3 years, 20%
   free-space headroom, and growth from the measured figure when it exists, otherwise the proxy.
   All are editable on the page.
2. **Which figure to buy against.** Recommendation: the **upper bound**. Buying against the lower
   bound risks running short if the size-sharers turn out to be unique.

## Out of scope

- Prices, brands or product recommendations: capacities and shapes only.
- Estate-wide numbers before U6 (the report says so in its header).
- Measuring embedding or captioning throughput (it uses configured figures; K3 and K6 measure).

## Handoff

One gate run and one independent check by a non-author verifier on the real T480, with F: and the
user roots catalogued. The verifier reconciles F:'s totals with Windows, checks the bounds against
the 2026-10-11 measurements, confirms 0 file reads and no quarantined names, and reports the
preliminary purchasing shapes the report gives. Counts only, never names.
