# M2-5 — Compliance dossier export (EU AI Act / ISO 42001 / NIST AI RMF)

## Goal
One-click export mapping a project's runs, artifacts, and governance events to record-keeping
obligations: model inventory (garage), lineage per artifact (G0 fold), human-approval records
(signatures, human-only verb confirmations), incident log (wedges, failures, blocked events),
data-source register (ingestion + coverage). An R2-style fixed-shape manifest producing a sealed
M2-3 document.
